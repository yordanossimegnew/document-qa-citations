# Acme Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. They are rotated every 180 days, and the last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

## Access to production

Production access requires a hardware security key for every engineer. Access is granted for 8 hours at a time through the access portal and is logged.

## Incident response

Security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. Customers affected by a data breach are notified within 72 hours.
